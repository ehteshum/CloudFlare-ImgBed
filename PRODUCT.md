# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Individuals running a self-hosted platform for unlimited storage. The primary context is a single user (the administrator) uploading, managing, and retrieving files from their personal cloud, similar to Google Drive or Terabox.

## Product Purpose
A personal cloud storage and image hosting platform. It exists to provide reliable, unbounded self-hosted storage. Success is defined by fast, frictionless uploads and easy retrieval.

## Brand Personality
Unlimited, utilitarian, spacious.

## Anti-references
It should absolutely NOT look like a "generated AI platform". Avoid saturated AI tells like generic beige/sand backgrounds, clichéd tiny uppercase eyebrows above sections, or glassmorphism as a default. It should feel like a sturdy, real tool instead of an AI prototype.

## Design Principles
1. **Endless Capacity:** The interface should feel spacious and unbounded, emphasizing unlimited storage.
2. **Concrete Utility:** Prioritize high-density file management and pure functionality over decorative trends.
3. **Distinct Identity:** Reject AI templates. Maintain a grounded, solid structure that feels intentionally crafted, not prompted.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast for text (WCAG AA), clear focus states for keyboard navigation, and functional feedback for upload progress without overwhelming motion.
